Singalong

Pick a folder and start singing.

Each song lives in its own folder: an audio file plus either a song.json or a standard UltraStar .txt. Folders made by UltraStar Deluxe or USDB Syncer work as they are, with their cover and video.

Nothing is uploaded. Your files are read in this browser only.

Put short looping videos in a folder called backgrounds inside your song folder and they will play behind the notes.

Google Drive

First time? How to get a Client ID (about 5 minutes, free)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
  2. In APIs & Services, open Library, find Google Drive API and click Enable.
  3. Set up the consent screen (it may be called Google Auth Platform or OAuth consent screen). Choose External, leave it in Testing, and add your own Google account under Test users.
  4. Go to Credentials, click Create credentials, then OAuth client ID, and choose Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add this page's address: (and your GitHub Pages address too, if you use it). No redirect address is needed.
  5. Copy the Client ID and paste it below.

While the app is in Testing mode, Google shows an "unverified app" warning the first time and may ask you to sign in again after about a week. That is normal for a personal project. This app only asks for read-only access to your Drive and never uploads or changes anything.
Party night

Mic & timing

Sing along on the beat. If the colour fills a note late, raise this until it starts exactly with the note. It covers mic delay and speaker delay together: USB mics and Bluetooth speakers often need 150 ms or more.

Singing an octave higher or lower than the original counts as correct.

Tip: speakers leak into the mics. Hold each mic close to your mouth, and raise a player's gate if the song or the other singer scores points for them.
